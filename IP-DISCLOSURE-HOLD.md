# CRFTE Public Disclosure Hold

**Effective:** 2026-08-25

This public repository remains the historical/public research record for CRFTE. However, **new enabling engineering disclosures are on hold pending IP review**.

## Do not commit here

Until the disclosure history and filing strategy are reviewed, do not add:

- new exact geometries, dimensions, tolerances, winding/pole layouts, or manufacturable module details;
- unpublished timing, waveform, phase, commutation, plasma-conditioning, or current-drive optimization;
- unpublished experimental data, calibration detail, or performance-improving parameter sets;
- new manufacturing, materials, cooling, electrical, or integration methods that materially enable implementation;
- confidential grant/partner material, invention disclosures, claim charts, patent drafts, attorney communications, or NDA-covered material;
- any other new detail that could materially help a competent engineer reproduce, optimize, manufacture, control, or scale an undisclosed implementation.

## May remain public

- material already publicly disclosed;
- historical/falsification records;
- high-level status and non-enabling summaries;
- non-confidential literature review;
- public safety/claim-boundary documentation;
- material intentionally released after IP review or filing.

## Repository rule

> **Public by default is no longer the rule for new CRFTE engineering detail. New potentially protectable implementation work must begin in controlled private storage and be released publicly only by an explicit disclosure decision.**
