# CRFTE P4E — Budget and Facilities Template

> **Naming:** CRFTE is the legacy program identifier. P4 is the current Atmospheric Electromagnetic Propulsion architecture, and P4E is its grant-scale proof-of-physics experiment. The present mechanism does not require counter-rotating magnetic fields.

**Purpose:** sponsor-neutral planning template. Final amounts must be replaced with institutional quotes, salary/fringe rates, equipment ownership status and the target sponsor's indirect-cost rules.

## Six-month proof-of-physics budget structure

### Scenario A — host laboratory already owns major magnet and pulsed-plasma infrastructure

Indicative direct-cost planning range: **$85,000–$150,000**.

| Category | Planning purpose |
|---|---|
| Personnel | PI/Co-I effort, graduate researcher or research engineer, technician support |
| Flow-cell fabrication | dielectric/insulating test cell, fixtures, electrodes for baseline sustainer tests, flow hardware |
| Nanosecond pulser access | facility usage, maintenance, switching consumables, or rental |
| RF subsystem | RF source/amplifier access, matching network, couplers, probes, shielding |
| Magnet facility | 1.5–2.0 T electromagnet time, cooling/power usage, fixtures |
| Electrical diagnostics | HV probe, current transformer/Rogowski, impedance measurement, synchronized DAQ |
| Plasma diagnostics | optical emission, photodiodes; laser/electron-density diagnostics via host facility where available |
| Force / momentum measurement | calibrated mN-class balance or equivalent pressure/momentum diagnostic and isolation hardware |
| Thermal / flow diagnostics | thermocouples/fast temperature measurement, pressure transducers, flow metering; optional schlieren/PIV access |
| Safety / integration | interlocks, shielding, grounding, emergency stop, EMI controls, qualified operator support |
| Data / compute | DAQ storage, analysis workstation time, model validation |
| Materials / contingency | connectors, feedthroughs, insulators, replacement parts, 5–10% technical contingency |

### Scenario B — partial major-equipment acquisition is required

Indicative direct-cost planning range: **$180,000–$300,000+** depending primarily on magnet/pulser/RF and diagnostic acquisitions.

This scenario should be avoided if a collaborating university or national-lab facility can provide the expensive infrastructure, because the scientific question is better suited to a facilities-access / collaborative grant than to building an entire plasma laboratory from scratch.

## Preferred facilities profile

A host laboratory is a strong fit if it can provide most of the following:

- atmospheric-pressure nanosecond pulsed-discharge capability at tens of kHz;
- RF amplifier and impedance/matching capability;
- 1.5–2.0 T transverse-field electromagnet or equivalent access;
- synchronized high-voltage/current diagnostics;
- optical-emission spectroscopy;
- electron-density or conductivity diagnostic capability;
- controlled dry-air flow rig;
- mN-class force or sensitive pressure/momentum diagnostics;
- high-voltage and magnet safety infrastructure;
- personnel experienced with nonequilibrium plasma/MHD experiments.

## Personnel roles

### Principal investigator / faculty sponsor

- scientific responsibility;
- institutional compliance;
- plasma/MHD experiment oversight;
- sponsor reporting.

### CRFTE project lead

- system hypothesis and reduced-order models;
- experimental requirements and falsification logic;
- data-analysis participation;
- documentation and integration roadmap.

### Graduate researcher / research engineer

- experiment setup and calibration;
- synchronized diagnostics;
- test-matrix execution;
- uncertainty analysis;
- model-to-data comparison.

### Technical / facility support

- pulser, RF and magnet operation;
- safety interlocks;
- mechanical/flow-cell fabrication;
- instrumentation support.

## Budget logic reviewers should see

The proposal should emphasize that funding is concentrated on **measurement of the uncertain physics**, not on fabrication of a speculative aircraft.

Priority order:

1. plasma conductivity-time diagnostics;
2. RF-on/RF-off controlled comparison;
3. polarity-reversible MHD force measurement;
4. energy accounting and uncertainty analysis;
5. only then, follow-on electrodeless current-drive work.

## Not in the P4E budget

Do not charge this grant for:

- a flight vehicle;
- an 8 T aircraft HTS magnet;
- airworthiness certification;
- full T.A.R./A.R.C. autonomy hardware;
- production propulsion modules;
- passenger systems;
- speculative vehicle manufacturing.

Those belong to later phases only if P4E passes its physics gates.
