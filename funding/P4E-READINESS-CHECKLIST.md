## Recommended submission posture

Use language such as:

> This project tests whether vibrational conditioning can improve the conductivity-time integral and electromagnetic momentum transfer efficiency of repetitively pulsed low-temperature atmospheric air. It does not presume vehicle-scale feasibility; the proposed measurements are designed to determine whether the underlying plasma/MHD mechanism merits subsequent scaling.

Avoid language such as:

- "working antigravity";
- "proven aircraft propulsion";
- "ready for flight";
- "breakthrough confirmed";
- unsupported novelty or patent claims.
