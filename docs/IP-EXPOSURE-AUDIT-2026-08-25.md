## 7. U.S. and international timing issue

The USPTO states that an inventor-originated public disclosure may fall within a **one-year U.S. grace period**, while public disclosure more than one year before filing can bar U.S. patenting. The USPTO also warns that pre-filing public disclosure may preclude patent rights in many foreign countries.

WIPO similarly advises that public disclosure through websites or other electronic means can destroy novelty in many jurisdictions, subject to jurisdiction-specific grace periods.

**Action:** do not calculate a filing deadline from this document alone. Patent counsel should determine the earliest enabling disclosure for each claim family and whether any non-GitHub disclosure predates the dates above.
