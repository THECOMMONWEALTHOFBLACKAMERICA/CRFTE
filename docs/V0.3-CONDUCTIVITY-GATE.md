# V0.3 P3 Optimization Gate

## Purpose

V0.3 is the next experimental campaign. Its purpose is not to generate thrust in the first test. It is to identify and optimize the moving-atmosphere plasma state most suitable for the later traveling-field electromagnetic momentum-transfer experiment.

V0.3 P3 replaces a single-point conductivity verdict with a controlled optimization map.

## Required measurements

- effective and, where diagnostics permit, spatial/temporal conductivity
- real electrical input to create and refresh the plasma state
- gas temperature and bulk temperature rise
- decay/persistence time
- airflow velocity and flow-field change
- spatial uniformity, streamer and filament behavior
- optical state
- repeatability and measurement uncertainty

## Diagnostic architecture

### Multi-length impedance

Retain 20/50/80 mm or host-lab-equivalent multi-length measurements so volumetric resistance can be distinguished from electrode/sheath/interface effects.

For a simplified bulk conductor:

```text
R_p = L / (σ A)
```

This relationship is a diagnostic model, not an assumption that the real plasma is spatially uniform.

### Spatial and temporal mapping

The preferred P3 output is a bounded map of conductivity behavior versus position and time rather than one scalar σ value. Diagnostic capability will be finalized with the host laboratory.

### Temperature and energy accounting

Track gas heating separately from electrical excitation. High conductivity that exists only through excessive thermal energy or localized arcing is not the desired non-equilibrium operating point.

### Flow mapping

Retain the 5×5 XY traverse where practical and compare plasma-OFF and plasma-ON flow fields.

## Optimization variables

Within facility-approved limits, evaluate the sensitivity of the conductive state to:

- excitation family and waveform
- deposited energy
- airflow (baseline comparison points 10/20/30 m/s)
- interaction geometry
- diagnostic position / downstream distance
- pulse timing / refresh behavior where applicable

Candidate excitation families for expert/laboratory evaluation include steady or pulsed DC, nanosecond-pulsed, inductive AC and capacitive AC. Selection and operating parameters are controlled by qualified laboratory personnel.

## Optimization scorecard

Do not rank configurations by conductivity alone. Rank the combination of:

- conductive volume
- uniformity
- persistence
- repeatability
- energy per useful conductive volume
- thermal penalty
- filament/arc fraction
- flow disturbance
- compatibility with later low-slip traveling-field coupling

The ~60–150 S/m range remains a hypothesis/engineering target. It is not a claimed experimental result.

## Iteration protocol

A configuration below the desired region triggers diagnosis and controlled iteration. Examples:

- filamentation → change excitation/geometry and characterize filling fraction
- rapid recombination/attachment → investigate persistence/refresh variables
- excessive heating → reduce thermal loading or change excitation mechanism
- uniform but low conductivity → explore approved excitation/geometry adjustments
- poor repeatability → resolve diagnostics or plasma stability before scaling

An individual configuration is not treated as a verdict on the whole research program. Results are nevertheless recorded without changing acceptance definitions after the measurement.

## Transition to V0.4

V0.4 begins after P3 identifies a sufficiently characterized operating point to justify electromagnetic coupling.

V0.4 must include:

- plasma-only baseline
- field-only baseline
- installed-hardware baseline
- combined plasma + field operation
- phase/direction reversal where the apparatus permits
- force/momentum measurement with uncertainty accounting

The purpose is to distinguish the intended electromagnetic contribution from ionic wind, thermal expansion, aerodynamic blockage, vibration and instrumentation bias.

## Power update rule

Once measurements exist, replace placeholder auxiliary assumptions with measured values:

```text
P_aux = measured plasma-generation power
      + measured drive losses
      + measured electronics losses
      + measured cryogenic/support losses
```

## Current program rule

> Optimize and characterize the plasma-flow state before selecting the V0.4 electromagnetic operating point. Vehicle-scale performance remains downstream of measured V0.3/V0.4 evidence.
