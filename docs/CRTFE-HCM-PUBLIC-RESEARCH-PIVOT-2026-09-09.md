# CRTFE-HCM Public Research Pivot — 2026-09-09

## Status

CRFTE remains an early-stage research program. No working lift hardware or flight vehicle has been demonstrated.

As of 9 September 2026, the immediate research priority has broadened beyond configuration-specific atmospheric-plasma conductivity inference toward a more general programmable electromagnetic-medium / constitutive-control research direction, referred to internally as CRTFE-HCM.

## Why the program pivoted

Technical review of the earlier atmospheric-plasma experiment highlighted two limits:

- electrostatic / air-breathing plasma propulsion already has a substantial research literature, so the broad propulsion mechanism is not by itself a new research direction; and
- conductivity inferred from one plasma-flow configuration may not generalize because plasma state, geometry, energy deposition, flow evolution, sheath behavior, temperature, and actuation method are strongly coupled.

The current research question is therefore broader: can an electromagnetic medium be programmed, measured, and controlled at the effective constitutive-state level in a reproducible, falsifiable way?

## Public scope

The public research direction focuses on:

- reproducible control of effective electromagnetic behavior;
- direct measurement and state estimation rather than extrapolation from one fixed plasma geometry;
- forward/reverse propagation tests and explicit calibration controls;
- identification of reachable versus unreachable commanded states; and
- separation of measured effects from component tolerance, drift, loss, and instrumentation artifacts.

The earlier P4/P4E atmospheric electromagnetic propulsion work remains part of the program's experimental and falsification lineage and may be revisited where stronger evidence supports it.
