# CRTFE V0.3 P3 — Laboratory Collaboration Brief

**Prepared for technical/laboratory discussion — 2026-08-25**

## Plain-language objective

We are trying to learn how to make a controlled volume of moving air electrically conductive in a repeatable and energy-conscious way before testing whether a traveling electromagnetic field can push that air.

We are not asking the laboratory to certify an aircraft or assume the propulsion concept works.

## What we need help with

1. Review the V0.3 P3 experiment design.
2. Identify which plasma excitation methods the facility can safely support.
3. Determine the best available conductivity/impedance diagnostics.
4. Measure how the plasma state changes across the duct and downstream.
5. Measure temperature, airflow and real electrical input at the same time.
6. Help establish uncertainty and repeatability.
7. Use the resulting map to select the operating point for a later electromagnetic force experiment.

## Starting apparatus

The initial diagnostic concept is a ~100 mm × 100 mm moving-air duct with 10/20/30 m/s comparison conditions, multi-length electrical measurements and an XY flow traverse.

Exact energized hardware, plasma source, protection, operating voltage/current/frequency and test limits are to be finalized with the host laboratory and qualified personnel rather than imposed in advance.

## Why multiple plasma methods matter

Different excitation methods can deposit energy into air in very different places and times. Therefore a conductivity number measured from one method/geometry may not predict another configuration.

The P3 campaign explicitly characterizes the available method rather than assuming all plasma sources are interchangeable.

## Measurements sought

- conductivity / impedance versus time
- spatial distribution to the extent instrumentation permits
- decay / persistence downstream
- real electrical input
- gas temperature / heating
- airflow and pressure change
- optical state / filamentation
- repeatability and uncertainty

## Optimization target

The best configuration is not simply the one with the highest conductivity. We want the best combined result for conductive volume, uniformity, persistence, energy cost, thermal load, repeatability and compatibility with later electromagnetic coupling.

The model-derived ~60–150 S/m region is an engineering hypothesis to investigate, not a claimed laboratory result.

## Next experiment after P3

If P3 identifies a sufficiently characterized operating point, V0.4 adds the traveling electromagnetic field and a force/momentum measurement system. Plasma-only, field-only, hardware-only and combined-operation controls are used to isolate the mechanism.

## Collaboration scope

A useful collaboration could range from experiment-design review through supervised multi-day laboratory testing. The working planning budget remains approximately $25,000 for a controlled V0.3 campaign, but final scope should be based on facility capability, required diagnostics, personnel and test time.

## Requested meeting outcome

Leave the meeting with:

- a technically defensible plasma-source choice or shortlist
- a diagnostic plan
- required facility/equipment list
- safety/qualified-operator requirements
- estimated laboratory time
- rough cost/scope
- next technical owner/contact
- enough information to freeze the V0.3 P3 test matrix.
