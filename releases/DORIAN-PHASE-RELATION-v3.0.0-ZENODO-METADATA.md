## Abstract

This work develops the Dorian Phase Relation beyond its weak second-order form for a local sinusoidal space-time modulated continuum. On the phase-uniform regular subluminal branch, the exact homogenized magnetoelectric coefficient is reduced to a Fourier-space carrier Schur complement and a positive first-return expansion with explicit modulation grading. The construction establishes vanishing odd total modulation orders, an exact phase-harmonic ceiling at each even order, nonnegative harmonic coefficients, and an all-orders phase-uniform truncation theorem: every finite even-order truncation has its maximum absolute phase-dependent remainder at the in-phase condition. Exact unequal-depth and higher-order formulas, an O(20) sign audit, and finite-ladder numerical continuum-bridge data are included as reproducibility supplements. The theorem is limited to the stated local sinusoidal continuum model and does not extend by itself to arbitrary waveforms, finite staggered ladders, lossy/dispersive devices, near-luminal multiband regimes, or hardware.
