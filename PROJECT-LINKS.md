## Project Status

As of **9 September 2026**, CRFTE / CRTFE remains an early-stage research program. No working lift hardware or flight vehicle has been demonstrated.

The immediate public R&D priority has broadened from configuration-specific atmospheric-plasma conductivity inference toward **CRTFE-HCM**, a programmable electromagnetic-medium / constitutive-control research direction.

## Accuracy Rule

The repository distinguishes models, simulations, conceptual vehicle art, proposed experiments, engineering plans, and measured results. A simulation is not a flight test, a blueprint is not a working aircraft, and a mathematical feasibility region is not experimental validation.
